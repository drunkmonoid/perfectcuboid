Usage: perfectcuboid [bricks | unbounded | rules | saunderson | legs | face | edge | check | nearmisses | stats | hunt | bench]

Search for perfect cuboids, Euler bricks and their near misses.

Options and flags:
    --help
        Display this help text.

Subcommands:
    bricks
        Euler bricks with every edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    unbounded
        Euler bricks by smallest edge up to LIMIT (default 1000), other edges unbounded.
    rules
        Check the known Euler brick rules on every brick with edges up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    saunderson
        Saunderson's Euler bricks with every edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    legs
        Factorise N (default 1000) and list every leg it pairs with.
    face
        Face cuboids whose whole face diagonals meet at an edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    edge
        Edge cuboids whose irrational edge is at most √LIMIT (default 1000).
    check
        The seven lengths of the box A × B × C, of any size, and how near perfect it is.
    nearmisses
        The first body, face and edge cuboid.
    stats
        How the number of Euler bricks grows, up to 10^EXPONENT (3 to 9, default 6).
    hunt
        Long search to FILE up to smallest edge UPTO. Ctrl+C stops, rerun resumes.
    bench
        Time the readable, fast and parallel searches up to LIMIT (default 100000).
