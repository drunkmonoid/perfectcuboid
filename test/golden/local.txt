What every primitive brick must look like, from trying every remainder mod a prime power:
  mod 2^8
    Euler bricks:    one edge divisible by 16, another by 4
    perfect cuboids: the same
  mod 3^4
    Euler bricks:    one edge divisible by 9, another by 3
    perfect cuboids: the same
  mod 5^2
    Euler bricks:    one edge divisible by 5
    perfect cuboids: the same
  mod 7^2
    Euler bricks:    nothing
    perfect cuboids: one edge divisible by 7
  mod 11^2
    Euler bricks:    one edge divisible by 11
    perfect cuboids: the same
  mod 13^2
    Euler bricks:    nothing
    perfect cuboids: some edge or the space diagonal divisible by 13
  mod 17^2
    Euler bricks:    nothing
    perfect cuboids: some of the seven lengths divisible by 17
  mod 19^2
    Euler bricks:    nothing
    perfect cuboids: one edge divisible by 19
