Usage: perfectcuboid [bricks | unbounded | rules | saunderson | legs | face | crosscheck | parallelepiped | edge | check | local | derived | curve | origins | graph | fourd | closest | nearmisses | stats | hunt | merge | verify | analyse | families | densities | records | misses | forecast | nearcalls | pictures | bench | scaling]

Search for perfect cuboids, Euler bricks and their near misses.

Options and flags:
    --help
        Display this help text.

Subcommands:
    bricks
        Euler bricks with every edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    unbounded
        Euler bricks by smallest edge up to LIMIT (default 1000), other edges unbounded.
    rules
        Check the known Euler brick rules on every brick with edges up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    saunderson
        Saunderson's Euler bricks with every edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    legs
        Factorise N (default 1000) and list every leg it pairs with.
    face
        Face cuboids whose whole face diagonals meet at an edge up to LIMIT (default 1000).
    crosscheck
        Face cuboids with space diagonal up to LIMIT (default 10000), found two ways, compared.
    parallelepiped
        Perfect parallelepipeds, slanted boxes with 13 whole lengths, with edges up to LIMIT (default 300).
    edge
        Edge cuboids whose irrational edge is at most √LIMIT (default 1000).
    check
        The seven lengths of the box A × B × C, of any size, and how near perfect it is.
    local
        Rules every Euler brick and perfect cuboid obeys mod primes up to P (default 37), by computer.
    derived
        Primitive Euler bricks with smallest edge up to LIMIT (default 100000), paired by derivation.
    curve
        New Euler bricks from the brick A × B × C, as multiples of points on elliptic curves.
    origins
        Which primitive bricks, smallest edge up to LIMIT (default 100000), come from formulas.
    graph
        The graph on 1 to LIMIT (default 10000) joining x and y when x² + y² is a square.
    fourd
        Four-dimensional Euler bricks and near misses from bricks with smallest edge up to LIMIT.
    closest
        The primitive bricks, smallest edge up to LIMIT (default 100000), nearest to perfect.
    nearmisses
        The first body, face and edge cuboid.
    stats
        How the number of Euler bricks grows, up to 10^EXPONENT (3 to 9, default 6).
    hunt
        Long search to FILE, up to UPTO. Ctrl+C stops, rerun resumes.
    merge
        Join hunts' FILEs over ranges that follow on from each other into a new file OUT.
    verify
        Check a hunt's FILE: every find again with BigInt, the checkpoints, a few blocks.
    analyse
        Counts, rules, origins and closest calls of the Euler bricks in a hunt's FILE.
    families
        Saunderson's family and those built from it, against the bricks in a hunt's FILE.
    densities
        How often small primes divide the edges of the bricks in a hunt's FILE, against prediction.
    records
        Records among the bricks in a hunt's FILE, and bricks per edge against its legs.
    misses
        Where the space diagonals of the bricks in a hunt's FILE fall between whole numbers.
    forecast
        What the primitive bricks in a hunt's FILE predict for carrying it on to UPTO.
    nearcalls
        The face or edge cuboids in a hunt's FILE whose irrational length is nearest a whole number.
    pictures
        Write SVG charts to DIRECTORY, from the bricks up to 10^EXPONENT (default 6).
    bench
        Time the readable, fast and parallel searches up to LIMIT (default 100000).
    scaling
        Time the search of EDGES edges (default 1e6) from FIRST (default 999000001) on 1 thread, 2, and so on.
