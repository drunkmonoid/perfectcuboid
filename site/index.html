The perfect cuboid

Is there a box whose three edges, six face diagonals and space diagonal are all whole numbers? Nobody knows. This project looks for one in plain, purely functional Scala 3, and keeps track of everything it finds on the way.

The biggest search so far went through every primitive Euler brick with smallest edge up to 10⁹ (a brick whose face diagonals are whole, but not yet its space diagonal): 10,220 of them, and no perfect cuboid.
